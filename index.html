03 — The House

Built for the city that knows its burgers.

Burgerhood is a neighbourhood kitchen with a single focus — serve burgers the way they should be served. Fresh buns, hand-pressed patties, sauces made in-house, and the kind of attention to detail that only comes from caring about every order.

Located in the heart of F-7 Markaz, we're a short walk from Margalla Road and a quick ride from most of central Islamabad. Come hungry, leave loud.
